Terms of Service Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how the Discord bot (the "Bot") collects, uses, and protects your information when you interact with it.

1. Information We Collect

To provide gameplay features, the Bot may store the following data:

  • Discord User IDs – to associate your game progress, inventories, and settings with your account.
  • Server IDs and Channel IDs – to manage server-specific configurations and game states.
  • Game-related data – items, currencies, pets, cards, achievements, cooldowns, settings, and other progress information generated through normal use.
  • Command usage timestamps – for cooldowns and rate limiting.

The Bot does not collect:

  • Passwords, payment information, or credentials.
  • Personal conversations or message logs. The Bot may temporarily process message content to respond to commands, but that content is never stored.

2. How We Use Information

We use stored data only to:

  • Operate, maintain, and improve the Bot’s game features.
  • Persist your game state across sessions.
  • Enforce cooldowns and prevent abuse.
  • Provide user support.

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to the Bot’s core functionality.

3. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal data to third parties. Data may be disclosed only:

  • When required by law (e.g., to comply with a valid legal request).
  • To protect the rights, property, or safety of the developer, users, or the public.

The Bot relies on Discord’s API. Data processed through Discord is also subject to Discord’s Privacy Policy.

4. Data Retention

We retain stored data as long as needed to deliver the Bot’s features. If you stop using the Bot, some data may persist until you request its deletion. Backups may retain data for a limited period but are purged in the normal course of operations.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access – request a copy of the personal data we store about you.
  • Deletion – request that your stored data be permanently deleted.

To exercise these rights, contact us using the details below. We will process requests within a reasonable timeframe, subject to any technical limitations (for example, data may briefly remain in automated backups).

6. Security

We implement appropriate technical measures to protect stored data from unauthorized access, alteration, or destruction. However, no method of electronic storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Children’s Privacy

The Bot is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or the applicable digital consent age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us so we can delete it.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be announced through the Bot or our support server. Continued use after the changes take effect indicates your acceptance of the updated policy.
